## Evening update

- **Studied:** bandwidth vs throughput. Bandwidth is the capacity of the link (the width of the road); throughput is what actually gets through (the cars that really pass). Throughput drops because of congestion, a bottleneck such as Wi-Fi interference, or an end device that can't keep up. Checked with a real case: a 300 Mbps plan measuring 120 Mbps on Wi-Fi.
