## 🎯 What I studied

Two talks by David Bombal with Cisco people who helped build the CCNA v2.0 (Cisco Live, June 2026), read through their transcripts.

**The verb in each exam topic tells you the question format:**

| Verb | What you face on the exam |
|---|---|
| Describe | Multiple choice, definitions |
| Diagnose | You get command output (e.g. `show interface` with CRC errors or late collisions) and name the **likely cause**, no fixing |
| Interpret | You read a **routing table** and say where a packet goes (longest prefix, AD, metric) |
| Validate | Compare a diagram with `show cdp neighbors`: does the documentation match the network? |
| Configure / Troubleshoot | **Lablets**: a small live lab on a device console, usually early in the exam |

**What changed from v1.1:** networking basics (router vs switch, TCP vs UDP, topologies), QoS, WLC configuration and shallow automation moved out; DNS troubleshooting (`nslookup`, A/CNAME records), secure device management (SSH, SCP/SFTP, AAA client config), client connectivity on Windows/Mac/Linux, and a 10% domain on **evaluating AI output** came in. Troubleshooting is at help-desk/L1 depth, not CCIE.

## 🔧 What I changed in my plan

- **Keyboard speed-runs:** every *configure* topic ends with rebuilding the same config from scratch in Packet Tracer, timed, until I get it right twice in a row without notes. Access ports, 802.1Q trunks, LACP and OSPF have to be automatic before exam day.
- **Diagnosis drills:** one piece of command output per session: "what's the likely cause?"
- **AI-output review:** I get configs with one deliberate mistake, as if an AI wrote them, and find it. That's domain 5 in practice.
- **Data hygiene:** labs use made-up addressing only; no real work configs go into an AI prompt.
- **Lab format:** one task, up to 5 devices, no answers given, one hidden fault. I paste `show running-config` and get it graded.
- Looked into **CML Free + Claude via MCP** (the AI builds the lab and grades it). It needs more RAM than my machine has, so Packet Tracer for now and a RAM upgrade before the routing phase.
